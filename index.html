About Bansal Institute of Engineering and Technology

Bansal Institute of Engineering and Technology (BIET) is a premier institution dedicated to providing quality education in engineering, technology, and management. The institute provides modern infrastructure, laboratories, experienced faculty, and excellent learning facilities for students.
