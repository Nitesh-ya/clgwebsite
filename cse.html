About Bansal Institute of Engineering and Technology

Bansal Institute of Engineering and Technology (BIET) is a premier institution dedicated to providing quality education in engineering, technology, and management. The institute offers modern infrastructure, well-equipped laboratories, experienced faculty, and excellent learning facilities to help students achieve academic and professional excellence. BIET focuses on innovation, research, skill development, and ethical values, preparing students for successful careers and lifelong learning.

Computer Science and Engineering Department

About Department

The Department of Computer Science and Engineering provides quality education in computer science, software development, artificial intelligence, and emerging technologies. The department focuses on innovation, research, and skill development to prepare students for successful careers in the IT industry.

Vision

To become a center of excellence in computer science education and research by developing skilled professionals.

Mission

  • Encourage research and innovation.
  • Develop industry-ready professionals.
